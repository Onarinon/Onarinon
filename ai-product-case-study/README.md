# SignalBrief — AI Product Case Study

> Turn customer feedback into evidence-linked opportunity briefs for human review.

**Author:** Arın · **Perspective:** Business Analyst / Product Owner · **Status:** Concept and delivery plan · **Version:** 1.0, 27 October 2026

## At a glance

| Area | Proposal |
|---|---|
| Problem | Small product teams manually group feedback and struggle to trace summaries to original evidence. |
| User | PMs and business analysts at small B2B software companies. |
| MVP | Upload CSV → review suggested themes and source quotes → edit → approve → export a brief. |
| Value hypothesis | Reduce synthesis effort without handing prioritization decisions to AI. |
| Primary target | At least 30% lower median time to an approved brief, subject to quality and safety gates. |
| Plan | Eight weeks from discovery to a controlled pilot, contingent on capacity and evidence. |

## Evidence and ownership

This independent portfolio case is hypothetical. No shipped application, employer engagement, interviews, production data, pilot results, or measured impact are claimed. Personas, estimates, and targets are assumptions to validate. Examples are synthetic.

The case demonstrates problem framing, requirements engineering, MVP decisions, acceptance criteria, delivery planning, and measurement. Implementation and validation are future work.

## Key product decision

Build evidence-linked synthesis before automated prioritization. AI suggests themes; users inspect, correct and approve them. Feedback frequency does not establish revenue impact or strategic priority.

CSV input keeps the first release bounded but introduces preparation effort. Human review improves accountability but consumes time. Both costs are included in the evaluation.
